- **Body sections:**
  - Life and era (10th-11th century Persia, political instability, patronage)

  - Major works (*Qanun*, *Shifa*, *Isharat*)
  - Influence on later philosophy (Ibn Turka, Mulla Sadra)

  - How Ibn Arabi developed it
