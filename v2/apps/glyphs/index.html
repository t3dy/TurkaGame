Twenty-six signs, no font

Until this page, every symbol in the project was a Unicode character, which renders as an empty box wherever the viewer’s font lacks it — and the alchemical block does. Each of these is drawn as a path: it needs no font, never renders as a box, scales to any size, and takes the hand it is drawn in. Toggle the hand above to see the same forms cut on a dark table and on an engraved page.

What these are. The standard forms — the barred triangles, the planetary signs as early modern printers cut them, the tria prima, the twelve sigils — drawn in a single-weight line the way an engraver would. They are my drawings of those forms, not reproductions of any particular manuscript or plate; the forms are centuries old and belong to nobody. The public-domain manuscript and woodcut grounds they are meant to sit on — creatures, zodiac animals, the elements in motion — are the half of the artwork not yet made, and would come through this repo’s rights-gated Commons pipeline.
