## The claim

MK names a seachange **the Occult-Scientific Revolution**: Ibn Turka's "totalizing
project became source code for the Mughal (Indo-Timurid), Safavid and Ottoman
Empires… predicated on the empiricist mathematization of the cosmos (mathesis)"
(*Prologue*). *Investigations* (1420) is offered as the **"opening anthem of the
Scientific Revolution, Islamic and otherwise"** — two centuries before the textbook
version, and continuous with it: Kepler, Bacon, Galileo and Newton figure as Ibn
Turka's "fellow radical Pythagoreanizers and indirect heirs."

## Fixed points (held)

- **The empirical anchor is the observatory**: the Mafāḥiṣ "coincided exactly with
  the construction of the Samarkand Observatory (1420) and the inception of its
  epochal star-tables" — theory and instrument in the same year, same network.
- **The parallel canon**: Ibn Turka and Kepler as "the greatest writers and readers
  of the two most mathematically accurate star-tables ever produced before telescopic
  observation, Ulugh Beg's and Tycho Brahe's respectively" (*Prologue*).
- **The Bruno standard**: the "persecuted visionary overwhelmingly vindicated after
  death" arc "alone should make him a Saint of Science too in the textbook Scientific
  Revolution story" — the double standard IS the argument (see
  [OCCULTOPHOBIA.md](OCCULTOPHOBIA.md)).
- **Mongol-era seed** (*Meanings*): Ibn Manẓūr's definition of siḥr — "morphing a
  thing into other than its proper self" — called the "[s]eed of the twentieth-
  century 'Scientific Revolution' story"; Ṭūsī supplying the philosophical legs.
- **Mathesis marries the Beautiful**: the revolution's Persian branch "was always
  meant to marry the True to the Beautiful" (*Prologue*) — form-is-content as a
  scientific value, not a literary one.

## Contested / open

consensus — the game's seals give us the machinery to do exactly that.
