# VISUALART.md — Art as Science: Painting, Calligraphy, Architecture

**Grimoire page, 2026-09-02.** Grounded in *Prologue* (the art-historical claims),
*OC* (Nujūm al-ʿulūm), docs/VISIONARY_ENVIRONMENTS.md, and this project's own
image-facing subprojects (yusuf-ascent, visionary-gallery, the portal image catalog).

## The claim

In the Timurid synthesis **art is science's twin, not its decoration**: "Because Art
is Science and Time is Tricksy, Persianate book painting also suddenly boomed during
the Timurid period" (*Prologue*). Ibn Turka's favorite proof-text (Q 38:45) is read
as "the marriage of Science and Art as the central human mission… the marriage of
Eye to Hand" — and his sensory theory puts **Sight above Hearing**, inverting the
Aristotelian-Avicennan order (⚠ full argument in "Vehicle of Total Perception,"
forthcoming; cited *Prologue* n.27).

## Fixed points (held)

- **The miʿrāj frontispiece boom**: paintings of the Prophet's Ascent "began to
  figure as manuscript frontispieces within a few years of our Timurid philosopher's
  death, and became a standard Safavid, Mughal and Ottoman visual idiom" (*Prologue*)
  — the Herat Miʿrājnāma of VISIONARY_ENVIRONMENTS is this exact wave.
- **The album preface** as a genre that "explicitly embodied Ibn Turka's
  ontogrammatology" (*Prologue*) — art criticism as lettrist metaphysics.
- **Architecture as text**: the Shaykh Luṭf Allāh Mosque's "monumental quranic
  calligraphy and blazing lines of Persian poetry are quite explicit that Hearing is
  ontologically superior and therefore epistemologically inferior to Sight" — the
  building argues the sensory doctrine.
- **The patron-artists**: Iskandar's atelier and Bāysunghur the calligrapher
  (*Prologue* n.34); the Deccan *Nujūm al-ʿulūm* as the most gorgeously illustrated
  Persian book, "almost exclusively imperial and occult" (*OC*).
- **Dībācha** (*Prologue* n.29): the word for prologue = silk brocade — "a
  beautified face, a beautified garden and a beautified preface ontologically
  equivalent."

## What it gives the game

- The image-heavy subprojects (yusuf-ascent, visionary-gallery) now have their
  doctrine page: every plate the games show is, in-world, an *epistemic instrument*.
  Captions can say so.
- The Elevation-of-Sight doctrine is a ready encounter (pivot_sensory exists —
  "Against Avicenna on the senses" — and now has its scholarly anchor); the
  witness's illustration layer (built, uncalled) is this theme's mechanical home:
  readers attaching images = the period's own text-image marriage.
- Anti-pattern: illustration as flavor. If a plate can't be tied to the scene's
  claim (or labeled as ours), it doesn't ship — the registry rule already enforces
  provenance; this page adds *function*.
