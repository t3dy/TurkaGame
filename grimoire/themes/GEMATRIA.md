# GEMATRIA.md — Abjad, Jafr, Taksīr: The Number Side of the Letter

**Grimoire page, 2026-09-02.** Grounded in *Prologue* (whose translated prologue and
notes are a live demonstration of the techniques), *Meanings*, BIOGRAPHY. Terminology
note up front: "gematria" is the *Hebrew* cousin's name; the Arabic apparatus is
**abjad** (letter-values), with jafr (⚠ the prognostic letter-science — term from
general knowledge, not in held texts) and taksīr (attested, *Prologue* n.35) as
operations. MK's texts say "alphanumericism" / "lettrist equation."

## The claim

Letter-number equivalence is not decoration but the **proof procedure** of lettrism:
where the Latin alphabet's "alphanumeric-algorithmic virtues are strictly partial,"
Arabic is "more perfectly alphanumeric, algorithmic and hence cosmic" (*Prologue*) —
so calculation on words is calculation on the world.

## Worked examples held in the corpus (all *Prologue*)

- **ṢWAB = 99**: "the Gate of Truth (ṣawāb = ṢWAB = 99)" keyed to the 99 divine
  names — and the ḥadīth's "one hundred save one" read as a numerical instruction
  ("whoso *counts* them shall enter Paradise").
- **ʿAṭā (gift) = 80 = aʿdād (numbers)** (n.46): "that this 'gift' is ontologically
  'Numbers' is signified by their shared value of 80."
- **ʿAlī (ʿLY) = 110 = alif (ALF)** (n.47): ʿAlī as avatar of the letter A and the
  One — "yet 110 gematrically reduces to Two, the value of B… per his famous
  declaration: I am the Dot under the B." Unity occulted within duality, as
  arithmetic.
- **The root-trinity engine**: ʿDD / ʿWD / ʿBD ("to number is to return is to
  serve") — semantic branching from consonantal roots doing "heavy epistemological
  and ontological lifting"; ʿabd = badʿ < badīʿ tying servanthood, creation and
  literary artifice into one root-play.
- **Taksīr mechanics** (n.35): letternames written in full, repeats eliminated,
  zubur/bayyināt split — the algorithm behind "the occult code behind every manifest
  word."
