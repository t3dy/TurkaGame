## The claim

The Safavids did not break with Timurid occult-imperialism; they **institutionalized
it under Twelver auspices**, with working occultists as the transition's managers:
ʿAlī Ṣafī's oeuvre was "expressly designed to help create out of the chaos of the
Qizilbash conquest a seamless Timurid-Safavid Sunni-Shiʿi continuum" (*OC*).

## Fixed points (held)

- **Confessional engineering** (*OC*): Sunni sources organized "within a
  thoroughgoingly imamophile framework" — the 5-5-12 / 3-12 / 2-12 structures
  honoring Those of the Mantle and the Twelve Imams; Kāshifī's *Rawżat al-shuhadāʾ*
  becoming the Twelver shrine-pilgrimage standard "under Safavid auspices… to the
  present." The scholarly labels ("Twelver Sunnism," "Alid loyalism,"
  "imamophilia") are catalogued at *OC* n.13.
- **Philosophical continuation** (*Prologue*): the Mafāḥiṣ "was a major source for
  Mīr Dāmād (d. 1631), fellow Isfahani architect of the successor Safavid state and
  Kepler's contemporary" — "the most important Western philosopher of Time
  (metatime) of the early modern era," likewise erased for the "stylistic crime of
  Pythagorean-lettrist ontological ornateness."
- **New Isfahan** as Pythagoropolis; the Shaykh Luṭf Allāh Mosque as built
  Planet-Pearl-Peach (see [EMPIRE.md](EMPIRE.md), [PSYCHEDELIC.md](PSYCHEDELIC.md));
  Ibn Turka's hometown becoming "the City of the Moon (ʿAlī)."
- **The Safavid recension of *Qasimian Secrets*** expands Kāshifī to all five Quintet
  sciences and reads as "a who's who of courtly occult culture" c. 1600 (*OC*).
