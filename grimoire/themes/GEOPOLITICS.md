**Grimoire page, 2026-09-02.** Grounded in full local *OC* (the imperial-continuum

## The claim

After the Mongol conquest, a single **Persianate imperial cultural continuum** —
"Timurid, Aqquyunlu, Safavid, Uzbek, Ottoman and Indo-Timurid (Mughal) Empires,
together constituting a new Chinggisid-Persian-Islamic world order" (*OC*) — ran on a
shared cosmology: "all embraced a specifically Timurid Pythagorean-Platonic
astrological-lettrist platform as default imperial cosmology and political science."
Geopolitics in this world is **cosmology plus logistics**: rival states, one operating
system.

## The load-bearing points (held texts)

- **The variable is the polity, not the doctrine.** Military rivalry rode on
  "cultural sociopolitical and philosophical continuity" — Kāshifī's *Treasury of
  Epistolography* eagerly received "at all early modern Persianate imperial courts
  (Safavid, Mughal, Ottoman, Uzbek)" (*Prologue* n.23). This is the portal's
  `ottoman-asymmetry` argument and v2's Assay mechanic in embryo: courts differ in
  what they *do* with the shared platform.
- **Scale**: the platform's reach ran "from the eastern Mediterranean and East Africa
  to the Strait of Malacca and the gates of China" (*Prologue*); Ulugh Beg's tables
  were used "from the Strait of Malacca to London to the nineteenth century."
- **Cairo as alembic** (*OC*): the New Brethren were "born in Mamluk Cairo around the
  turn of the fifteenth [century], which cosmopolitan capital served as central
  alembic for the experimental testing of new forms of occult-philosopher kingship" —
  and "this specifically Cairene ethos helped drive, ironically, the Ottoman conquest
  of the Mamluk Sultanate," ⚠ via Kemālpaşazāde's "lettrist call for the conquest of
  Cairo" (chapter title held in 2021 TOC; text unread).
- **The Sunni–Shiʿi transition managed by occultists** (*OC*): ʿAlī Ṣafī's oeuvre
  "expressly designed to help create out of the chaos of the Qizilbash conquest a
  seamless Timurid-Safavid Sunni-Shiʿi continuum" — imamophile Sunnism ("Twelver
  Sunnism," "Alid loyalism") as the confessional solvent.
- **War-tech dimension**: bazm u razm magic is military R&D — armies of smoke, courier
  ciphers, interrogation tech, campaign provisioning (*OC* translation). The Deccan
  *Nujūm al-ʿulūm* marries "Greece and India, Persian and Sanskrit" with "innovative
  emphasis on magic war tech."
- **The comparative frame**: Zarakol's *Before the West* is the cited scaffold
  (*OC* n.4) — this is a story about "Eastern world orders," not a periphery of
  Europe's.

## What it gives the game

- **The empire-steering verbs**, straight from the sources: legitimate a dynasty
  (court_dynasty, philosopher-king arguments), set campaign dates (court_razm_date),
  cipher the couriers (court_razm_device), manage a confessional transition
  (unbuilt — the Ṣafī move: repackage the doctrine for the successor regime's
  legitimacy; a strong Phase V/legacy encounter), and *export the platform* (the
  system fates' `source_code` is precisely "six court cultures adopt it").
- CareerSim Phase III's three courts are a correct miniature of the continuum;
  the roguelike's floors-as-polities (v2 descent) is the full thesis.
- Anti-pattern: never write inter-court difference as doctrinal war. The war is
  logistical/dynastic; the doctrine travels freely across the front lines.
