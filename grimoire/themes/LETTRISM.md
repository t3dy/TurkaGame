# LETTRISM.md — ʿIlm al-Ḥurūf, the Science of Letters

**Grimoire page, 2026-09-02.** Grounded in *Prologue* (the fullest local statement),
*OC*, *Meanings*, docs/BIOGRAPHY.md, the portal's `lettrism-universal` argument entry
(turka.db), and the v2 engine's own derivations. ⚠ not held: "Of Islamic
Grammatology: Ibn Turka's Lettrist Metaphysics of Light," *Al-ʿUṣūr al-Wusṭā* 24
(2016): 42–113 — the deep-dive; citation exact.

## The claim

Lettrism is **"Islamic kabbalah"** (*Prologue*'s own gloss) — with Hebrew kabbalah a
"pan-Mediterranean twin sister" of common late-ancient Greek alphanumeric descent —
and in Ibn Turka's hands it becomes the first **mathematical-theurgic philosophy** of
the Greater West: the doctrine that the Arabic alphabet is the cosmos's source code,
and its manipulation therefore simultaneously mathematics, physics and worship.
*Investigations* is "the most philosophically systematic formulation of lettrism…
ever penned."

## Fixed points (held)

- **The wellspring is the muqaṭṭaʿāt**: the Quranic cut-up letters are "the
  wellspring of lettrism" (*Prologue*), and lettrism itself "the endless splitting of
  language to release mathematical consciousness, like spirit from body… the most
  literal possible emulation of the Cut-Up Letters" (n.25).
- **The lineage as Ibn Turka built it**: ʿAlī (as Pythagoras redivivus, inventor of
  letter science) → Ibn ʿArabī and Ḥamūya *paired as co-founders* — "he is the first
  to add Saʿd al-Dīn Ḥamūya… as co-father" (*Prologue*); the game's
  `lineages_declared` flag is exactly this act.
- **The universality argument** (portal `lettrism-universal`): only the letter
  encompasses being and non-being; lettrism therefore outranks philosophy's
  being-qua-being. This is the claim the tribunals ultimately try.
- **The technical layer** (*Prologue* n.35): taksīr (cognate to Hebrew temurah) —
  splitting a word, writing letternames in full, eliminating repeats; zubur (first
  letters of letternames) vs. bayyināt (the remainder) — "the occult code behind
  every manifest word." Divine-name mantras (awrād) timed by number as "central
  lettrist technology."
- **The three registers** (*Prologue*): Written/Spoken/Mental as Pearl/Peach/Planet —
  already the v2 engine's execution model (plan / run-once / persist).
- **Science of the Imams**: lettrism as "the science of the Imams par excellence"
  (*OC*) — why imamophile Sunnism and lettrism travel together.

## What it gives the game

- v2's charter ("the world is written in letters") is *Prologue*-exact; the portal's
  `three-lettrisms` and the rulesets' motive-differences stay the canonical frame.
- The muqaṭṭaʿāt encounter (isfahan_study_two) is the doctrine's origin moment and
  now grants the rank — correct. What's missing anywhere: **taksīr as a playable
  operation** (split, expand, reduce — a real algorithm, teachable as a puzzle;
  natural for Letter Machine or a CareerSim demonstration).
- Anti-pattern: lettrism as symbol-matching. Every lettrist act should have an
  arithmetic the player could check (the abjad values are in the game's own data).
