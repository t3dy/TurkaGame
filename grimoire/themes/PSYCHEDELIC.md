# PSYCHEDELIC.md — "Psychedelic" as a Term of Art in Melvin-Koushki

**Grimoire page, 2026-09-02.** Grounded in the full local *Prologue* (where the term
does load-bearing work at least four times) and this project's own reckoning with the
word (docs/PIVOTS.md P1; games/yusuf-ascent). ⚠ marks unverified attributions.

## The claim

MK uses **psychedelic** not as 1960s color but as a precise descriptor for a period
aesthetic-epistemic strategy: prose, painting and architecture engineered to *alter
the reader's or viewer's state* so that form performs content. It is his English for
the effect of ontological ornateness — language or ornament so dense it becomes the
mind-transporting operation it describes.

## The instances in hand (all *Prologue*)

- The Mafāḥiṣ prologue "is a **psychedelic mantra and talismanic machine itself**" —
  the thesis sentence of the whole essay.
- Ibn Turka is "the first author in both Persian and Arabic to marry gorgeous,
  tricksy, prosimetric, **psychedelic style** to philosophical-scientific argument";
  his and Bruno's shared "authorial ethos and crime: text as sorcery as starship."
- Timurid book painting's "frequent **psychedelicality** gave birth to a new Safavid
  literary genre, the album preface, which explicitly embodied Ibn Turka's
  ontogrammatology."
- The Shaykh Luṭf Allāh Mosque is "overwhelmingly, **psychedelically** gorgeous… the
  most architecturally and visually literal possible incarnation of *Investigations*'
  Planet-Pearl-Peach series ever built."
- Kindred vocabulary does the same work: Ibn ʿArabī's prose is "mindbendingly
  theurgically ornate"; Ḥamūya's is "jazz-philosophical" (via Uy); the muqaṭṭaʿāt are
  compared to Burroughs's Cut-Up Method — "language is a virus from outer space"
  (*Prologue* n.25).

Note the mechanism claim hiding in the term: these artifacts are *technologies of
state-change* — the mosque's acoustics "for divine-name mantra recitation," the
prologue as mantra, the miʿrāj frontispiece as launch diagram. Psychedelic = the
artifact does to you what it depicts.

## Contested / open

The word is rhetorically hot, and this project already burned a hand on it:
**PIVOTS P1** records that building Yūsuf Ascent to a curator's "psychedelic
out-of-body philosophy" line would have produced "lava-lamp Orientalism," and the fix
was the period's own operative vocabulary — ʿālam al-khayāl, barzakh, miʿrāj, khalʿ.
The two positions are compatible once sequenced: MK earns "psychedelic" *after* the
philology, as a translation of an effect; the game must do likewise — the term is a
conclusion, never a brief. Related: Erik Davis's *High Weirdness* is cited approvingly
in *OC* n.23 ("trauma and high weirdness are twins"), tying the register to his
weird-studies alliance.

## What it gives the game

- License and limit for the visionary environments program
  (docs/VISIONARY_ENVIRONMENTS.md): psychedelic effect via period mechanism only —
  ornament density, script-into-tendril transitions, acoustics, ascent framing. If a
  shader can't name its mechanism from a page, it's mood, not research.
- The album preface as genre is unmined: a late-phase composition option where the
  *preface* to a picture-album carries the doctrine (ontogrammatology smuggled as art
  criticism). Low exposure, high transmission — a genuinely different writing choice.
- The VN's Shaykh Luṭf Allāh-adjacent aspiration: the Planet-Pearl-Peach reversal
  (Pearl→Peach→Planet as built) is a ready-made perspective/ordering puzzle in the
  Station family (v2 reckoner).
