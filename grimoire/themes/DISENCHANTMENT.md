# DISENCHANTMENT.md — Against the Disenchantment Story

**Grimoire page, 2026-09-02.** Grounded in *Meanings* (the frontal statement) and
*OC* (the modernity inversions). The Weber word itself doesn't appear in the held
texts; this page names the thesis MK attacks by its usual academic name, and says so.

## The claim

The story in which modernity progressively strips magic from the world is, on MK's
evidence, **backwards twice over**: the "disenchanted" categories were manufactured
by identifiable colonial-confessional actors (see [OCCULTOPHOBIA.md](OCCULTOPHOBIA.md)),
and modernity itself is saturated with exactly the operations the grimoires codify —
"modernity" as "the mafioso mass episteme-laundering racket" (*Meanings*).

## Fixed points (held)

- **The inversion catalogue** (*OC*): nation-states and corporations as "imagined
  communities that terraform, linguaform, mentiform and bioform"; "democratic
  societies ensorcelled by social and other media are… increasingly stage-magical";
  "much of global modernity, by ʿAlī Ṣafī's definition, remains just as devoted to
  deepfakery and warlord magic." Reagan's court astrologer (n.5) as the punchline.
- **AI as the new occult machine** (*Meanings*): "Magic Talking Statues and Silicon
  Golems… these fantastically hungry occult machines (even their creators have no
  clue how they work)"; the Comprehensive Prognosticon as imperial computer —
  "shades of the LLM craze." The Algorithm "has exclusively been trained" on the
  West-and-Rest story.
- **Psychophysics as re-enchantment with a lab coat** (*Meanings*): "regreeking
  'magic' as psychophysics — the study of mind-matter interactions — instantly
  elevates that slur… to the sacred abode of Science"; the archive as "the dataset
  motherlode… for the mind-matter relationship over the longest of longue durées."
- **The therapeutic claim**: what's missing now is "the Poetic Will to Wonder at the
  Weird for Partycrashing Pleasure as… a philosophical, ethical and therapeutic
  [imperative]" — the licit/illicit siḥr distinction offered as "guide to a less
  horrorshow poiesis" (*Meanings*).

## What it gives the game

- The game's frame-story opportunity: the player's own screen is one of the "occult
  machines." The manual's history folio could close on this inversion — one line,
  no lecture (MK's Bezos/Prometheus footnote shows the register: a dated fact,
  dryly placed).
- Design guard: the game must never play disenchantment's tune in reverse either —
  no "the magic was real all along" wink. The sources' position is subtler: the
  *operations* are real technologies of perception and state; keep outcomes inside
  that envelope (which the bands already do — illusions persuade, inks cipher,
  nothing teleports; ʿAlī Ṣafī himself excludes flight and invisibility "as beyond
  reason," *OC*).
