## The claim

Star science and letter science are **one program run on two instruments**: "it is
only with the Great Weirding that was the Mongol conquest that star science and
letter science began to boom in tandem" (*Prologue*), and astrology is "our species's
favorite technology of time [and] also its single most extensively recorded empirical
mind-matter program" (*Meanings*) — predicated, note, "on the personhood of planets."

## Fixed points (held)

- **The astrolettrist merger personified**: Yazdī, "the first to make the science of
  history fully formally astrolettrist," working at the Samarkand Observatory
  (*Prologue* n.36); pseudo-Avicenna "rewritten as a bestselling astrolettrist
  grimoirist" (*Prologue*).
- **Selenocentrism**: for Ibn Turka, "Imam ʿAlī as inventor of Letter Science is
  Pythagoras is Moon is Panopticon is Mathematical Wormhole to Reality"; "Letters are
  Moons"; even Europe's Scientific Revolution story is "peculiarly Selenocentric"
  (*Prologue*). The Moon, not the Sun, is this cosmology's operative body.
- **Apogee/perigee as theology**: the opening prayer's "drawing near [that is His
  Perigee], negating the distance [of His Apogee]" — the Sufi's distress from the
  divine read as "the empirical astronomical fact of planetary apogee" (*Prologue*).
- **Astral magic as Persianate default**: taskhīr "used in the first place for astral
  magic from the Mongol era onward… since planets are people too" (*Meanings* n.8).
- **Longevity of the instrument**: Ulugh Beg's tables in use to the nineteenth
  century; "even Ronald Reagan had his court astrologer" (*OC* n.5).
