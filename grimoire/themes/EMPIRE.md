# EMPIRE.md — The Occult Science of Empire

**Grimoire page, 2026-09-02.** Grounded in full local *OC* and *Prologue*; the theme's
namesake works are ⚠ not held: "Early Modern Islamicate Empire: New Forms of
Religiopolitical Legitimacy" (Wiley-Blackwell *History of Islam*, 2018, 353–75) and
the forthcoming *The Occult Science of Empire in Aqquyunlu-Safavid Iran* (citations
exact from *OC* n.5 and the 2021 volume's contributor note).

## The claim

Empire in the post-Mongol Persianate world is an **occult-scientific artifact**: its
legitimacy engineered by astrologer-lettrists, its capitals planned as talismans, its
histories written astrologically, its wars provisioned by grimoire. "Nowhere did
occult science fuse with magical state more fully than in the post-Mongol, early
modern Persianate imperial cultural continuum" (*OC*).

## The load-bearing evidence (held)

- ***Investigations* as "imperial engine"** and "source code for the Mughal
  (Indo-Timurid), Safavid and Ottoman Empires and smaller states alike, inspiring
  everything from cosmology, poetry, architecture and armor to observational
  astronomy, music, mind-control magic and predictive computing" (*Prologue*).
- **The Comprehensive Prognosticon as imperial computer** (*Meanings*): the
  Persianate Pythagoreans "perfected the Comprehensive Prognosticon as imperial
  computer — shades of the LLM craze as we speak."
- **The king's body as talisman** "uniting and healing heaven and earth"; court
  ritual and imperial architecture "quite explicitly occult" (*OC*).
- **Isfahan as Pythagoropolis** (*Prologue*): Safavid New Isfahan as "the world's
  first purpose-built Pythagoropolis, Solomonic-Hermetic city-as-talisman-and-
  observatory and so Islamic New Atlantis"; the Tāj Maḥal marrying Sun to Moon.
- **Bureaucracy as second Book**: Kāshifī's *Treasury of Epistolography* as an
  "imperial-bureaucratic matrix" that rewrites "the Court and the State as a Second
  Book" (*Prologue*) — the chancery itself lettrist.

## What it gives the game

- CareerSim's Imperial reputation and the `source_code` fate carry the thesis; the
  unbuilt piece is **state-scale composition**: a city plan, a chancery manual, a
  prognosticon as artifacts distinct from books-for-readers. The Slice-3 workbench
  should have one state-artifact track.
- The roguelike's floors-as-metaphysics (v2 descent) can grade into floors-as-
  imperial-institutions: chancery, observatory, atelier, army — one science doing
  four kinds of imperial work.
- Anti-pattern: empire as backdrop. In every court scene the state should be
  *consuming* the science on-page — a table, a date, a legitimation, a cipher.
