## The claim

The talisman is the continuum's **unit of applied science** — "devices that conjunct
active celestial forces with passive terrestrial ones to produce effects weird and
wondrous" (*OC*'s translation of ʿAlī Ṣafī's definition of līmiyā) — and it scales
without changing kind: an inscribed ring, a book, a king's body, a city. "Talismanic
books, bodies and buildings abounded across Islamo-Afro-Eurasia to a degree never
before seen" (*OC*).

## Fixed points (held)

- **The scaling series** (*OC*, *Prologue*): amulet → *Investigations*' prologue as
  "talismanic machine" → the king's body as "a talisman uniting and healing heaven
  and earth" → New Isfahan as "city-as-talisman-and-observatory." One physics, four
  sizes.
- **The mechanism is conjunction**: active celestial + passive terrestrial — which is
  why talismanry (līmiyā) and astrology are one curriculum, and why the Ṭahawī Circle
  can be simultaneously diagram, proof and device.
- **Historical potency claim** (*Prologue*): the Mongol conversion to Islam turning
  on "the gift of [Ḥamūya's] father's talisman and robe" — a talisman at the hinge
  of world history, in the tradition's own memory.
- **Political talismans** (*OC*): ʿAlī Ṣafī's Amulet of Protection titles itself as
  protection *from the trials of time* — the talisman as career insurance for
  bureaucrats ("safety… from every stripe of fear or danger… freedom from all forms
  of restriction and incarceration").
- **The Būnian layer** (portal corpus): al-Būnī's diagrammatic tradition — the
  magic-square/divine-names apparatus — as the Arabic substrate the Persianate
  synthesis draws up ⚠ (relationship characterized from portal syntheses; the
  Gardiner chapters are held but not re-read this session).
