## The claim

The erasure of Islamicate occult science is not an oversight but a **colonial
construction with named architects**, and the remedy is not "inclusion" but a
methodological inversion: use the Arabo-Persian archive as the *control case* for
understanding early modernity, because it is larger. *Meanings*: "On empirical
grounds alone, Arabic and Persian must be used as control for Greek and Latin based on
the proportionality of surviving sources… to understand the globalizing,
mathematizing, algorithmizing early modern era."

## The method's moves (all evidenced in held texts)

1. **Name the spell to break it.** "Magic" is "that Greco-Latin anti-Persian slur" —
   an etymological weapon refracted through Latin/Greek, Christian/Pagan,
   Protestant/Catholic, Christian/Islamic binaries (*Meanings*). The genealogy runs
   through named actors: Zwemer's missionary polemic ("In no monotheistic religion are
   magic and sorcery so firmly entrenched…", 1920) → Glidden at the Library of
   Congress, making occultophobia "the official classification principle" of the
   archive itself.
2. **Archive arithmetic.** Islamicists "by rights should have at their disposal two
   orders of magnitude more" manuscripts than Latinists; the ratio of sources to
   permitted specialists is "precisely inverted — which inversion AI is not just
   encoding as law but exacerbating massively" (*Meanings*).
3. **Restore the period's own categories.** Not Magic-as-master-category (which
   "never became" one in Islam) but the Occult (khafī) and the Weird (gharīb) —
   ʿulūm gharība among the classified sciences (*Meanings*, *OC* introduction's
   two-fold jaliyya/khafiyya division, translated in full).
4. **Comparative rehabilitation by pairing.** Ibn Turka read as "a Muslim Iamblichus
   and Cusanus and Pico and Bruno and Dee… considerably more influential than all
   these Christian peers" (*Prologue*) — the pairing forces the double standard into
   view. See [DEE.md](DEE.md).
5. **Positive renaming.** Persian Pythagoreanism as icon of mathesis "to decolonize
   the modern historiography of science" (*Prologue* n.4); regreeking magic as
   "psychophysics" to move it from Religion's ghetto to Science's abode (*Meanings*).
6. **Institution-building as praxis.** The graduate programs in Magic (Exeter, USC),
   Societas Magica, the MOSAIC ERC project remarrying Islamic and Eastern Christian
   sources in five languages (*Meanings*) — decolonization done as infrastructure.
