## The claim

The comparison is bidirectional and load-bearing: both men pursued "mathematical,
magical, kabbalistic/lettrist, alchemical, and anagogic unification of knowledge in
service of a millenarian, one-world empire; both smeared in their own day, both more
influential after death" (notes/01). But the *asymmetry* is the point: Ibn Turka's
program "paid off" immediately — three patrons, an observatory, an imperial cosmology
— where Dee's patronage bids "all failed, and he died poor with his library
destroyed." Same species, opposite ecosystems: the Islamicate world funded what
Christendom starved. See [OCCULTOPHOBIA.md](OCCULTOPHOBIA.md) for why the
historiography then inverted the verdict.

## The concrete parallels (held)

- **Sigil-summaries**: the Ṭahawī Circle (Majlis MS 10196, f. 63a) as "the Islamic
  answer to the Hieroglyphic Monad" — each man compressing a life's system into one
  drawable figure (notes/01).
- **The Bruno inversion** (*Prologue*): the "dramatic story arc as persecuted
  visionary overwhelmingly vindicated after death is classically Brunian… But in a
  poetically perfect inversion of the modern hagiography of Giordano Bruno… Dr.
  Littleturk has instead been historiographically burnt at the stake."
- **The counterfactual court**: Murad III as Sufi-initiate prophet-saint-king devoted
  to oneiromancy/astrology/talismanry; the *Book of Soyga* argued to derive from the
  Būnian-Bistāmian corpus (notes/01) — Dee's own angelic materials already carry
  Islamicate fingerprints.
- **The Cusanus control** (*Prologue*): fellow lawyer, shared Pythagoreanizing
  vision, five centuries to canonization, "no observatories" — patronage ecology,
  not genius, decides outcomes.
