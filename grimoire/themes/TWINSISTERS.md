## The claim

Melvin-Koushki calls Hebrew kabbalah lettrism's "pan-Mediterranean twin sister,"
of common late-ancient Greek alphanumeric descent, and notes that **taksīr is
cognate to Hebrew *temurah***. That is a genealogical claim about vocabulary. This
page records something sharper that fell out of reading the two traditions'
technical layers side by side for the letter-powers reference: **they do not merely
resemble each other thematically. They repeatedly produce the same STRUCTURE with
different contents** — and the structures, not the contents, are what a block game
can be built out of.

Three parallels, all held, and each one is a mechanic.

## Fixed points (held)

### 1. Twelve letters are twelve faculties — in both

- **Arabic.** Al-Būnī's twelve "spiritual letters" are "not letters in a
  conventional sense. Rather… they represent the human faculties" (Gardiner p. 56,
  from *Laṭāʾif* f. 18a): hearing, vision, smell, taste, touch, the cogitative,
  imaginative, formal, administrative, integrative, preserving and dispositive
  faculties. The five external senses plus the Avicennan internal ones.
- **Hebrew.** The *Sefer Yetsirah*'s twelve simple letters each take one of twelve
  human actions — "sight, hearing, smell, speech, taste, coition, action, motion,
  anger, laughter, thought, and sleep" (Segol p. 41).

Same count, same job, **different anthropology**: al-Būnī's twelve are the
*sensorium* of Avicennan psychology; the SY's twelve are *actions and passions*,
including anger, laughter and sleep. One tradition divides the mind's machinery;
the other divides a life's doings.

### 2. Both mediate power through a middle tier of twelve

Al-Būnī's cosmology is three-tiered and the middle tier is load-bearing: "the
powers of the seven luciform letters do not act directly on the corporeal letters,
but rather only through the mediation of the twelve spiritual letters that are the
human faculties—much as the powers of the planets are mediated by the signs and
houses" (Gardiner p. 57). Seven above, twelve between, twenty-eight below.

The SY's counts rhyme: three mothers, **seven** doubles (planets), **twelve**
simples (signs and faculties), twenty-two letters. Seven planetary, twelve
zodiacal, in both — and in al-Būnī the twelve are explicitly the *human* term
through which the seven reach matter, with the sharp corollary that the reach
depends on the person: "every human cosmos in accordance with his worthiness to
witness the lights of those letters."

### 3. Both put a cube at the centre of space

- **Hebrew.** SY15–16 give the six directions, which the medieval commentators
  draw as a "space cube" and read as "the six dimensions of the throne of glory"
  (Segol p. 91); the six are *sealed with the divine name*. Then SY38 does it
  again for the doubles: seven is "the six sides of a cube with the Holy Temple
  set in the middle, with God 'supporting them all'" (Segol p. 72).
- **Arabic.** No cube, but the same instinct toward a closed, exhaustive spatial
  set: twenty-eight letters = twenty-eight lunar mansions = twenty-eight parts of
  the body, because twenty-eight is a perfect number (de Callataÿ pp. 24–25), and
  at any moment "fourteen of them are visible above the earth and fourteen are
  below" (Varisco p. 502, translating *Shams* f. 4r).

The Hebrew structure is **static and spatial** (a cube you stand inside); the
Arabic one is **rotating and temporal** (a wheel half above the horizon). Same
impulse — partition the whole and give each letter its place — resolved once as
geometry and once as a calendar.

## Where they genuinely differ, and it matters

- **The SY has no earth element.** Three mothers, three elements: air, water,
  fire (Segol p. 23). Al-Būnī's letters are divided by four qualities in seven
  degrees (Gardiner p. 57), and the corporeal letters are "like the earth in
  relation to the spiritual letters." A Hebrew world has nothing to stand on that
  is not made; an Arabic one is grounded by definition.
- **Assertion versus demonstration.** The Ikhwān "do not explain how these
  parallels may be accounted for… They just find them natural" (de Callataÿ p. 25).
  Ibn Turka's whole contribution is the refusal of that: lettrism as *proof*, by
  proportion, on philosophy's own ground (portal `lettrism-universal`). The
  twin-sister resemblance is at the level of technique; the intellectual ambition
  is not shared, and that asymmetry is the reason the intellectual ruleset exists.
- **What the operator is for.** The medieval golem "is not a proper subject… it is
  used to effect metaphysical changes, including the resurrection of the dead and
  the reconstruction of the cosmos" (Segol p. 105). The Islamicate counterpart in
  *Boon for the Khan* is a putrefaction rite yielding a winged chimera whose blood
  is a *material* with tabulated properties (fire-walking, water-walking,
  reanimation) — an instrument on the shelf, not a cosmic act.

## What it gives the game

- **The twelve-faculty middle tier is the strongest unbuilt structure we hold.**
  Power reaching matter *only through* a faculty, and only as far as the operator
  is fit, is a dependency graph with a sourced motive. It gives the sage a stat
  that is not "level," and it gives a puzzle a shape: you cannot act on the block,
  you can only act on the faculty that acts on the block.
- **Seven doubles = six faces + centre.** The most directly implementable spatial
  fact in either tradition, and in a cubic-cell engine it is a cell and its six
  neighbours, exactly. Nothing in v2 or GoldenDawnBlocks uses it yet.
- **The rotating half-alphabet is the turn scheduler both the roguelike and
  CareerSim were missing.** Fourteen letters above the earth and fourteen below,
  advancing one per night, with the dot-omen scale as the cost — and the dot counts
  in `v2/data/letters.json` already match al-Būnī's fifteen-and-thirteen exactly.
- **A cross-alphabet mode is legitimate, not a crossover gimmick.** Because the
  structures line up at seven / twelve / a closed spatial set, a level can pose the
  same task in both alphabets and let the difference in *content* — sensorium
  versus deeds, cube versus wheel, earth versus no earth — be the puzzle. That is
  the honest version of what `../../../GoldenDawnBlocks/` was scaffolded for.
- **Anti-pattern this page exists to forbid:** treating the twin-sister claim as
  licence to merge the two into one syncretic system. Melvin-Koushki's claim is
  *common descent*, not identity; Segol's tradition and al-Būnī's disagree about
  the elements, the operator, and what a demonstration is. Ship them as two
  rulesets that share an engine — which is exactly what the vendored-engine
  arrangement already is.
