## The claim

"Pythagorean" is MK's chosen banner — "everywhere explicit and implicit in my
Persianate sources… as icon of mathesis to decolonize the modern historiography of
science" (*Prologue* n.4). The thesis: Ibn Turka is "the first thinker in the Greater
Western tradition to develop a mathematical-theurgic philosophy, very consciously
Pythagorean and very consciously Modern, that successfully challenged the hegemonic
Aristotelian-Avicennan system" — the **mathematization of the cosmos ran through
Persia first**.

## Fixed points (held)

- **ʿAlī as Pythagoras redivivus** (*Prologue*): the tradition's own genealogy —
  "identified in this tradition with a reincarnated Pythagoras" (BIOGRAPHY), inventor
  of letter science, "the Dot under the B."
- **The Tetractys made lettrist**: the Ṭahawī Circle as "the first Modern lettrist
  version of the Tetractys"; the Chain of Being as "Unit Numbers as Necklace as
  Jewelstrung Rosary as Highway to Heaven" (*Prologue*).
- **The Bruno resonance**: MK's retranslation exercise — minor adjustments make
  Bruno's *De triplici minimo* "express Ibn Turka's Arabic philosophy," proving "He
  errs not who says Pythagoreanism — Iranian and Italian — is one" (*Prologue*).
- **Music and mantra**: the Pythagorean "Lute," divine-name mantras as planetary
  prayer, base-ten planetary prayer as "Cosmic Rosary as Tetractys" (*Prologue*) —
  the harmonic side the game's `harmonic_link` flag already touches.
- **Modern framing**: "Our current technocratic, algorithmic Euro-American moment can
  be cosmologically styled Platonic-Pythagorean too" (*Meanings*) — the frame cuts
  both ways, at us.

## What it gives the game

- The Quintet-as-verbs design and v2's derive-don't-assign rule are Pythagorean in
  MK's exact sense: number and form doing the work, doctrine earned from observables.
- Unmined: the **musical octave** as content — Cairo's samāʿ scene gestures at it;
  a demonstration that letter-ratios and string-ratios agree (the `harmonic_link`
  memory) deserves its payoff encounter in a later phase.
- Anti-pattern: "sacred geometry" vagueness. The Tetractys here is a *diagram with a
  manuscript shelfmark* (Majlis 10196, f. 63a); always route through the artifact.
