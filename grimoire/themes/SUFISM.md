# SUFISM.md — The Sufi Question: Adjacent, Entangled, Not Identical

**Grimoire page, 2026-09-02.** Grounded in *Prologue* (the misreading critique),
*OC* (Naqshbandī occultists), BIOGRAPHY, notes. This page exists to police the
game's single most dangerous shortcut.

## The claim

Sufism and lettrism share personnel, lodges and vocabulary — but **calling Ibn Turka
"a Sufi mystic" is precisely the misreading MK's project exists to overturn**:
"most of the handful of modern accounts of Ibn Turka still reflexively paint him as a
generic Shiʿi Sufi mystic — exactly the charges he vehemently defended himself
against in his remarkable apologies and creedal statements" (*Prologue* n.7). The
relationship is real and must be *rendered as tension*, not identity.

## Fixed points (held)

- **Entanglement is deep and attested**: Qāsim-i Anvār is a charismatic Niʿmatallāhī
  master; ʿAlī Ṣafī and Kāshifī are Naqshbandīs (Kāshifī wrote "the definitive
  history of that stridently Sunni order," *OC*) who are also working occultists;
  Ibn ʿArabī and Ḥamūya come wearing Sufi honorifics. The khānqāh and samāʿ are real
  venues in Turka's Cairo (BIOGRAPHY).
- **The redescription move** (*Prologue*): Ibn Turka "subvert[s] the classic
  Persianate poetical tropes and their Sufi associations" — Plato's Cave rewritten
  "as Mine of Mind and Pearlbed of Being"; the Sufi's "subjective distress at
  distance from the divine presence" read as "the empirical astronomical fact of
  planetary apogee." Sufi language, scientific referent.
- **The danger runs through the association**: the tribunals' charge-sheet logic and
  Qāsim's 1427 exile ("for his lettrist associations," *OC* n.24) show mystic and
  lettrist blurring precisely where it *hurts* — in an accuser's hands.
- **Jazz-philosophy** (Uy via *Prologue*): Ḥamūya's prose as the deconstructionist
  extreme of Sufi writing — the stylistic bridge by which Sufi ornament became
  lettrist method.

## What it gives the game

- The game already encodes this correctly in places (the creedal-statement option at
  trial_first; the "generic mystic" misreading as historiographical fate) — this
  page makes it a standing rule: **Sufi contexts are staging grounds and liability
  vectors, never explanations.** An encounter set in the khānqāh should end with a
  lettrist point or a political cost, not a mystical glow.
- Unmined: Turka's own *apologies and creedal statements* as artifacts — the
  wrote_creed flag exists; the documents deserve first-class artifact status
  (composed, circulated, cited at later tribunals) [links: CREEDS.md — not yet
  written].
- Anti-pattern (binding): "mystical" as an adjective in any player-facing gloss.
