# IRAN.md — Iran as Center of Gravity

**Grimoire page, 2026-09-02.** Grounded in *Prologue*, *Meanings*, *OC*,
docs/BIOGRAPHY.md.

## The claim

In MK's cartography, Iran — the Persian cosmopolis with Iran at its core — is the
**center of early modern Western intellectual history**, not its periphery: "the vast
early modern Persian cosmopolis, encompassing (in extreme contrast to Latin) a good
half of the human race" (*Meanings*). The "Greater Western
(Helleno-Judeo-Christo-Islamic) tradition" (*Prologue*) is one tradition, and its
early modern mathematizing avant-garde wrote in Persian and Arabic.

## Fixed points (held)

- **The anti-Persian genealogy of "magic"** (*Meanings*): the category-word is "this
  ancient Greek anti-Persian xenophobic slur," refracted "now again [as] Greek versus
  Persian as emblem of knowing male West versus unknowing female East." The field's
  master category is a slur against the culture whose archive dominates the record.
- **The denial**: "such a thing as early modern Persian Pythagoreanism — my central
  philological and theoretical focus… — cannot, must not and therefore did not
  exist, as I am frequently told" (*Meanings*).
- **The Iranian lineage of the science** (held texts): Ṭūsī redefining takhyīl to
  make weirding-wording licit siḥr; the Maragha then Samarkand observatories; Ḥamūya
  as Ibn ʿArabī's "Iranian twin"; Isfahan, Herat, Yazd, Samarkand as the working
  capitals.
- **The modern wound** (*Prologue*): "endless Euro-American sanctions and wars" keep
  even tourists from Isfahan's monuments — access to the heartland itself is
  geopolitically constrained, compounding the archival erasure.

## What it gives the game

- The game's existence as an *Isfahan-centered* history of science is the thesis
  enacted. Keep place-specificity aggressive: the bench, the Yazd road, the Gulf's
  pearl-beds, the eastern fruit cities — the Prologue's own Pearl/Peach/Planet
  geography riddle (n.28) is a geography lesson disguised as a poem, and playable
  as one.
- Lexicon candidates: "Persian cosmopolis," "Greater West" — two framing coinages a
  player should meet with glosses.
