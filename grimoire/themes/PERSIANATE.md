# PERSIANATE.md — The Persian Cosmopolis as Analytic Frame

**Grimoire page, 2026-09-02.** Grounded in *OC*, *Prologue*, *Meanings*. ⚠ not held:
"Powers of One: The Mathematicalization of the Occult Sciences in the High Persianate
Tradition," IHIW 5/1 (2017): 127–99 — the frame's namesake study; citation exact
(cited in both *OC* n.3 and the 2021 volume).

## The claim

"Persianate" in MK is not an ethnic or linguistic label but a **civilizational
operating system**: the post-Mongol "Chinggisid-Persian-Islamic world order" (*OC*)
in which New Persian is the courtly-scientific lingua franca from Ottoman Europe to
Bengal, and the "High Persianate tradition" is its mathematized crest.

## Fixed points (held)

- **Language as infrastructure**: an Arabic text's siḥr "could simply become taskhīr
  when done into Persian for a South Asian audience" (*Meanings* n.8) — translation
  as re-engineering, since planetary subjugation (taskhīr) IS the Persianate frame's
  default magic. Kāshifī's Persian adaptations are the cosmopolis's shared
  curriculum (*OC*).
- **English as mirror** (*OC*, note on the text): "by accident of historical and
  imperial evolution New Persian and Modern English structurally mirror each other
  quite precisely" — hence MK translates "Persian as English and Arabic as Latin."
  A working rule this project's prose can borrow outright.
- **Vernacular hybridity**: "the host of hybridizing Islamicate vernaculars"
  (*Meanings*) surrounding the Arabic-Persian core.
- **Encyclopedism as Persianate signature**: bestsellers "synthesizing dozens to
  hundreds of other occult-scientific works" (*Prologue*) — the cosmopolis reads,
  digests and republishes together.

## What it gives the game

- CareerSim's Arabic/Persian composition axis (pivot_language) is the cosmopolis
  mechanic already; adopt the *OC* register rule for the game's own prose — Persian-
  coded surfaces read plain and warm, Arabic-coded read Latinate and formal.
- The audience question — who can read what, and in which tongue — is a Persianate
  question before it is a marketing one; every composition choice should say which
  public it reaches.
