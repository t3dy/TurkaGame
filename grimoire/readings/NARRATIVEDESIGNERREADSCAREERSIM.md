## Who Turka can approach — and who is missing

The cast as built (people.js) is sound: Akhlāṭī, Qāsim, Yazdī; the Isfahan pair
(deputy, copyist); the patron trio as an apparatus spectrum (TIMURID.md); plus
calligrapher, astronomer, rival, student — every one a capability package, none a
stat stick. The gaps, each grounded in a theme page:

1. **The fellow student who wins abroad.** ʿAbd al-Raḥmān al-Bisṭāmī — Akhlāṭī-circle
   contemporary who carried the letter-science to the Ottoman court ([[BISTAMI.md]];
   portal source held, unread-in-full). The living counterfactual: same master, same
   science, different polity, no tribunals. Grants {ottoman_channel, jafr_literature};
   his letters in Phases III–V are the GEOPOLITICS thesis wearing a face the player knows.
2. **The principled anti-occultist.** The Khaldūn-voice (GEOMANCY.md) — critique as
   *literature*, not jealousy. Distinct from the rival: he argues in public, cites
   honestly, and can be answered (Yazdī did). Debating him is the one road where
   Orthodox+ and Occult+ can move together.
3. **The bureaucrat clientele** — the vizier who buys "psychic control of tyrants"
   (PATRONAGE reading D4): patronage played from below, unbuilt.
4. **The cataloguer** — the proto-occultophobe who misfiles the living author's work
   (DEE reading D3): the double erasure's in-world seed.
5. **The household** — `trial_third`'s patron road puts "the household between you
   and the panel," but no person carries it (⚠ verify BIOGRAPHY's family-seat
   framing before naming one).

## How patron-working should deepen

The PATRONAGE reading stands; its ranking here: contract terms first

Built: the summa road (Synthesis is the spine); astrology's service ladder
(muwaqqit → nativities → dynastic → razm dates → zij). Thin: **geomancy** (one

*quote* it).

## How courtly interests differ, and how steering feels

Each court consumes the science differently, on-page (EMPIRE.md): Iskandar
*experiments* (tolerates INVENTED-edge, burns hot), Bāysunghur *collects* (artifact
quality), Ulugh Beg *proves* (mathematics or nothing), the judiciary *applies* (the
science's daylight face). Steering is then three verbs and a refusal — legitimate,
date, cipher, refuse — plus the attested fifth road, **the New-Brethren reframe**
(MILLENNIALSOVEREIGNTY.md n.51, GEOPOLITICS reading D1), the synthesist's privilege.
Steering is never a meter; it is read out of CourtMemory, circulation, and the Later Record.

## What he writes, and for whom

The readings converge on composition's axes: language (PERSIANATE.md's register
rule), cipher and index (GRIMOIRES reading D1–D2), pedigree (D4), state-scale
artifacts (GEOPOLITICS D2), creeds (above). The foreclosures matter more than the

careers*, and a run should be rememberable as its bibliography — the run's own
shelf belongs beside the Attested Life's Mafāḥiṣ and Circle.

## Mentors and students — the three arcs

- **Akhlāṭī's debt** runs *backward*: the master dies early, and the debt is what
  the player does with a heterodox inheritance — claim the lineage
  (`circle_lineages`; declaring Ḥamūya co-founder is Turka's own novel act,
  LETTRISM.md), launder it into mathematics, or bury it. Needs one Phase V beat:
  the panel asks, by name, whose student he was.
- **Yazdī's copy** is built (`one_hand`, `pivot_yazdi_copy`, `yazdi_keeps`). The
  promotion HISTORIOGRAPHY.md licenses: the ending variant where the Chronicle the
  player watched being inked is revealed as Yazdī's hand (*Prologue* n.36 supports
  it). Cheap, true, devastating — the chronicle conceit earns its author.
- **The students** are the movement's future and its evidence (`trial_student_copy`,
  the curriculum). Missing beat: the succession question (`student_asks_the_future`,
  GEOPOLITICS D4) — may the work be recut for the next regime? A circle outlives a
  man exactly there.
