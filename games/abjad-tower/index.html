The Notebook

Taḥqīq, not taqlīd. A correspondence you have read is held on authority; one you have put to the test is verified. The notebook pays only for the second. Each claim below is a scheme the tradition — or this game — proposes; a claim is CONFIRMED only when a rival on the same question is DISPROVEN, so the play is finding the tower on which the schemes disagree. Shared across every game in this project.

Where the game bends its source. A letter's mass is its abjad value, but compressed: the raw series runs 1 to 1000, three orders of magnitude, which no physics solver stacks without exploding. build_letters.py takes log₁₀, so the order of the letters is exactly the tradition's and the ratio is ours. That is the only place the correspondence is altered, and it is stated in the generator, in the data file, and here.

Tool — the weight of brackets
The balcony brackets in the painting are carried on nothing, so here they are the only bodies that may be fixed in empty air. Every other piece of the folio is dropped and must be carried. A piece’s weight is its share of the page.
Tajriba — put the tower to the test
Every letter here has a nature — fire, air, water, earth — and complementary natures hold where opposed ones slide. Which letter has which nature is hidden, because the tradition does not agree; three rival schemes are in play and one of them is the physics. Build, watch, record. Each record is scored against every scheme in the Notebook.
