# ERRATA — what the frozen visual novel says that the corrected biography revises

**The game itself is frozen** (`games/FROZEN.md`) and was **not changed**: `choices.json`, `js/*.js`, `logs/` and the
archived `games/visual-novel-v1/`, `-v2/`, `-v3/` are exactly as they were. This file only records, by scene and choice id, where
the game's text or premise disagrees with the corrected biography, so that Ted can decide whether the VN is left as a
period piece, patched, or superseded (`TurkaVita/` is the biography-driven game built on the corrected sources).

Written 2026-09-27 under Ted's standing rule (always correct older documents to the most current information). Source of the
corrections: `TurkaVita/docs/CORRECTIONS_BRIEF.md` (row numbers below) and `TurkaVita/docs/TURKA_AUDIT.md`, backed by the 2012 Yale
dissertation (`SRC-610EE1D6BA`, pdf page = printed page + 17). The current life is `docs/BIOGRAPHY.md` (rewritten today).

**Reading key.** *Game says* quotes or paraphrases `choices.json` / `js/narrative.js` / `js/ui.js`. *Current* is the corrected
statement with its hedge. *Severity*: **premise** = the scene is built on it; **flavour** = a line of colour that could be
reworded; **label** = a choice tag or ending name.

## What is *not* wrong

The Occult Quintet, the Ṭahawī Circle as a diagram of the *Mafāḥiṣ*, Yazdī as best friend and foremost student, the *New Brethren of
Purity*, the Ibn ʿArabī / Ḥamūya pairing, the Cusa/Pico/Bruno/Dee comparisons (Melvin-Koushki's, not contact), and the "one ending
among several" framing are consistent with the corrected sources.
