Visionary Gallery

Twenty-two Persianate folios — ascension cycles, planetary pavilions, demons on bare paper, a book of omens, and six folios from the same manuscript as Bihzād's impossible palace — downloaded with their licences, measured, cut into parts, turned into printable paper tunnel books and into 3D, and made playable.

What the measuring is for.

The Yūsuf Ascent portal argues that Bihzād's palace is a diagram rather than a house, and the evidence offered is that "every surface is finished to the same degree of attention." That is not a metaphor — it is a claim about how detail is distributed across a page, and it can be tested. So it was. The measurement disagrees; the Method page says why, and why the disagreement is more useful than a confirmation would have been.
