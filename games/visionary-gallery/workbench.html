Each auto-proposed region becomes a flat quad whose depth is the layer the heuristic gave it. Positions and sizes are compensated about a station point by k = (D − z)/D, so from one privileged viewpoint the exploded stack projects to the identical image as the flat folio. Move at all and it comes apart.

The difference from Prototype B is the input: there the 43 elements and their seven rungs were drawn and argued by hand; here nothing was touched. This is the same mechanism with the labour removed, and it is visibly cruder. That comparison is the point.

A paper tunnel book

A tunnel book is a stack of cards held apart by concertina side walls, each cut away except for what belongs on its plane. It has exactly the property the 3D view was built to demonstrate — the picture only coheres from the front — and it is about three hundred years older.

The sheet is a single self-contained SVG: cards with fold-back tabs, two notched side walls, cut lines in red and folds in dashed blue. Print at 100%, cut, fold, slot together back card first.

Region boxes, not traced silhouettes — a box is what you can cut with a knife and a steel rule on a kitchen table.
