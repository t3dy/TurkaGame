Where the measurement contradicted the essay

1. "Every surface is finished to the same degree of attention."

That sentence is the load-bearing evidence in the Yūsuf Ascent portal for reading the palace as a diagram rather than a house. Operationalised as attention evenness — one minus the dispersion of detail density over a 16×16 grid — it does not single the Yūsuf folio out at all.

Why, and what it is worth. The metric punishes exactly the thing the portal itself praises: Bihzād's large blank white arch. The portal says he "reserves blankness for the exact place where the event happens" — a deliberate, enormous, low-detail region — and gradient density cannot tell a considered void from an unfinished one. So the disagreement locates a confound rather than refuting the claim. The honest statement is that uniformity of treatment and uniformity of detail density are not the same measure, and only the second one is implemented here. That correction is worth more than a confirmation would have been, and it is why the portal's sentence should not be quoted as though it had been tested.

2. "The machine layers agree with the hand-drawn rungs about two thirds of the time."

An earlier draft of this site said that. Nobody had measured it. Then it was measured, on the one folio that exists in both forms — 41 hand-drawn regions argued into seven cosmological rungs, against 27 regions the pipeline proposed with nothing touched.

What the machine misses.

So the split verdict is: the heuristic ranks well and finds badly. It reproduces the direction of the ascent on the regions it happens to catch, and it does not catch the figure, the halo, the chamber or the brackets — which is to say, it misses every element the argument is actually made of. An automatic pipeline gets you a corpus; it does not get you a reading.

Where this sits on the coupling ladder

The repo's CONTEXTENGINEERINGGAMEPIPELINES.md sets out five levels of coupling between the research and the game. The two things on this site sit at different rungs of it, on purpose.

L0
Transcription

Read a source, write a scene by hand. No pipeline; correctness lives in the author's head.

L1
A canonical research-access layer — Yūsuf Ascent

43 elements, seven rungs, eight door-locks, all hand-authored against a small curated layer and each labelled with how well it is grounded. Expensive per image, and it is the only one of the two that produces an argument.

L2
Structured DB → build-time export — built 2026-09-01

portal/scripts/export_gallery_scholarship.py now exports portal entries into data/scholarship.json: the entry text, literature and the portal's own confidence/review flags come from portal/db/turka.db at build time; only the tradition-to-entry links are authored here, and each shows its one-line rationale on the page. Two gaps the export surfaced (no divination entry; no painting-world figures) are recorded in the JSON rather than papered over.

L3
Fact-level provenance through to runtime

Half-built: every image here carries its licence and Commons record through to the page. Text does not yet carry claim ids.

L4
Data-driven generation — this gallery

Region proposals, layer assignment, papercraft nets and the 3D stack are all generated against measured properties of the source, not authored. 22 folios entered and none needed a writer. The stated failure mode of L4 — content that is individually well-formed and collectively gives a false picture — is exactly what the machine-versus-hand comparison above is for.

Honesty rules this site follows

  • Nothing whose licence did not parse as free was downloaded. The rights gate is in the fetch script, not in a human's memory. It had a bug on first run — a regex that rejected every plain pd file — which is recorded in the script's own comments rather than quietly fixed.
  • Measurements and heuristics are labelled differently everywhere. The metrics are measurements. The region proposals and the layer assignment are heuristics, and every surface that shows them says so.
  • Numbers on this page come from a script. Where a figure appears, it was read from hand_vs_machine.json or gallery.json at page load, not typed in.
  • Disagreements stay on the page. Both contradictions above could have been removed by deleting a sentence. They are the most useful thing here.
