The seven doors
Hover or select a piece to read what it is and what it does.
The rows are the seven rungs of the Yūsuf ladder — ours, not the painting's. Each piece's rule is the logic the painting already gives it: the stair reaches any storey because it belongs to none; the brackets stand on nothing; the cupola cannot be reached because no stair in the picture reaches it. The street door is where you stand, so it admits you; every other door is locked from within until the right thing is built.
