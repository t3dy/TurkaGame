## Other things worth the designer's notice

* **The dissertation contradicts itself on where he was when writing the Mafahis** (timeline: "Isfahan or Yazd?", pdf 52; ch. 3: Shiraz and Yazd 817-25/1414-22, pdf 177) and on how many Iskandar-court entries there are (two overlapping timeline entries, 812-15 and 815-17). Neither is resolved by a later paper.
* **Fażl Allāh Astarābādī's death** is 1393 in the dissertation's diagram A.2 (pdf 54), 796/1394 in its timeline (pdf 51), and 1394 in New Brethren (pdf 48). I did not write a claim on it (peripheral).
* The New Brethren paper (pdf 10) calls Ibn Turka Akhlati's *khalifa* to the Timurid east; the dissertation (pdf 66, 67) calls him Akhlati's chief disciple "in the east" and the Akhlati letter (pdf 163) addresses him as the luminary of the circle. Consistent; no claim written.
* **Hurufi charge**: the dissertation attributes the 830/1427 arrest to suspected Hurufi ties (pdf 73). ## Method notes

* Diacritics: the JSON follows the dissertation's spelling with diacritics; the generator maps ASCII forms to them, so any spelling drift is in the generator, not in a source.
* Only the artifacts' `content` paraphrases pages I opened. Quoted spans are kept short and verbatim; the build's quote check passed.
* Types used: `attested` for what a paper says (as reported by MK); `directly_inferred` for CL-0703; `inferential_reconstruction` only for the Prologue's "refuse to bend the knee" (CL-0701, low); discrepancy claims are `analytical_construct` (the schema allows it) with `confidence` medium or low, because they are statements about how two accounts relate, not about the world.
