the Tamhīd reception excursus), plus 95, 96, 97 (dating method and MS Majlis 10196 description), 50-54 (timeline

## Scope

- The entries are alphabetical by title. **Entry 25 (Nuqṭa) begins on pdf p.124 and runs onto p.125, so it is R3's**;
  my first entry is **26 Qābiliyyat** (heading on pdf p.125) and my last is **46 Żawʾ al-Lamaʿāt** (ends pdf p.149).
  §2.1.3 (correspondence) begins on p.150 and is not mine; I read p.150-151 only to see where the oeuvre ends.
- The brief listed Nafsat al-Maṣdūr I/II, Iʿtiqādāt/Iʿtiqādiyya, Mabdaʾ u Maʿād among "the major works ... as they
  fall". **They do not fall in this range**: alphabetically they precede Nuqṭa (no. 25), so they belong to R3's
  half. From my range the major works are the Sharḥ Fuṣūṣ al-Ḥikam, Sharḥ-i Naẓm al-Durr, Tamhīd al-Qawāʿid,
  Suʾl al-Mulūk, Shaqq-i Qamar, Sharḥ al-Basmala, Tuḥfa-yi ʿAlāʾī and Żawʾ al-Lamaʿāt.
  The Mafāḥiṣ, Munāẓarāt, Manāhij are likewise R3's; I cite them only where my entries cross-refer.

### The Majlis 10196 folios in manuscript order (for the collection-ordering key)

From my range only (R3's works fill the gaps, e.g. the Mafāḥiṣ f. 52a-118b, Nuqṭa ff. 124b-26a):
Naẓm al-Durr "-1b"-51b; ʿAmāʾ 119b-20b; Waḥda 120a-20b; Ḥadīth Nabawī 122a; Żawʾ al-Lamaʿāt 148b-53b; Shaqq-i Qamar
170b-72a; Suʾl al-Mulūk 172b-79a; Tamhīd 189b-"23a"; Kashshāf 223b-24b; Dah Bayt 225a-26a; Fuṣūṣ 241b-330a;
Basmala 332b-51b; Sullam 360b-66a; Tuḥfa 371b-73b + 375b-82a; Tarjuma 440b-41b.
Copy dates in that order run 827 (ʿAmāʾ, Waḥda), 828 (Naẓm, Żawʾ), 829 (Shaqq, Suʾl, Basmala later in the volume),
830 (Tamhīd), 831 (Fuṣūṣ), 832 (Sullam), then 838 (Dah Bayt at f. 225a, posthumous). So neither the folio order nor the
copy-date order is a single chronology; this is the shape of the problem MK describes on pdf p.96 (first half roughly
chronological, second half not).

**Do not treat these folios as an exact key without a check** (CL-0338): ʿAmāʾ (119b-20b) and Waḥda (f. 120a-20b) overlap;
the Naẓm range begins "-1b"; the Tamhīd end is printed "23a"; the Tarjuma is at 440b-41b in a manuscript MK gives as
425 ff. (pdf p.97, p.151); the Tuḥfa's 375b-82a overlaps the interjected Yazdī fragment MK places at 374b-75b (p.97).

Folios of the marginal letters (pdf p.151, not mine to encode) sit against my works: 122a (three letters; Ḥadīth
Nabawī), 148a and 153b (around Żawʾ), 172a (end of Shaqq-i Qamar), 51b (within Naẓm); the Qāżīzāda letter cited for
the Basmala is f. 153a (inside the Żawʾ range).

## Discrepancies and uncertainties (with pdf pages)

1. **Shaqq-i Qamar CE date, printed two ways** (p.126: 18 Rabīʿ I 829 = 27 February 1426; p.128: = 28 January 1426).
   A tabular-calendar check I ran (approximate, ±2 days, my own, not MK's) supports late January, not late February:
   every other Hijri/CE pair in my range agrees with MK's CE dates to within my calendar's constant offset, and only
   this one does not. Recorded in CL-0302 and `date.ce`; I did not pick.
2. **Sharḥ Fuṣūṣ: 814 or 813.** The entry (colophon f. 330a) says finished 20 Ṣafar 814/13 June 1411; the timeline
   (p.52) says 813/1411. CL-0311 (contested).
3. **Naẓm al-Durr dated 806/1404 but referencing later works.** The entry says completed Dhū l-Ḥijja 806/June 1404
   (p.135) and that it references both the Mafāḥiṣ (823/1420 on p.173) and the Sharḥ Fuṣūṣ (814/1411); MK does not
   remark on it. CL-0323 (`analytical_construct`, my observation, not MK's). "Cairo" is his presumption; the timeline
   (p.51) puts him in Cairo c. 795-810. The Arabic version (margins of MS 10196) and Persian are separate texts (fn. 56;
   fn. 55 on p.134 adds that Bihbahānī says Sipahsālār 2930/11 preserves the Arabic version), so which version 806
   belongs to is not stated.
4. **Basmala recipients.** Entry: copy sent to Qāżīzāda Rūmī, marginal dedication to Ulugh Beg (f. 332b). Timeline (p.52):
   copies sent to *both*. CL-0306 (contested).
5. **Tamhīd date/place.** Entry: asterisked, Shiraz is the copy's place. Timeline (p.52): "completes the Tamhīd in Shiraz"
   in 830/1427. §2.2 list (p.174): "(Shiraz, 830/1427?)". CL-0330 (contested).
6. **Sullam classification.** The entry (p.137) calls it a mystical exposition; the §2.2 list (p.175) also files it under
   "Apologies, creedal works". Whether the duress rule applies to it (as it would to `creed_tract`) is a design call; I used
   `work` for its content EV and recorded the classification in EV-0668 and CL-0328.
7. **MS Majlis 10196 extent and copying span** stated three ways: p.95 (last eight years of his life and three past his
   death), p.97 (copied 828-41/1425-37), p.151 (828-38/1425-35). The Dah Bayt copy (838/1435) is posthumous by any of them.
8. **Abarquh (Sullam, 832/1429).** The timeline's exile list (p.53) names Mazandaran, Gilan, Hamadan, Azarbayjan, Natanz,
   not Abarquh; his autograph note is at Chālū in Tabaristan on 28 Ṣafar 832 (Dec 1428). Not resolved.
9. **Shelfmark oddity.** "MS Arabic BIJ 1238/3", British Library (Bijapur copy, 869-71/1465-67), is listed as a copy of
   Nuqṭa (R3), the Naẓm al-Durr (no. 38, item 5) and the Tamhīd (no. 42, item 3), each as "/3"; one physical item cannot be
   all three, so either the entry is a majmūʿa listing or one shelfmark is a slip. Not resolved; not encoded.
10. **Gulshan-i Rāz attribution** is MK's "likely candidate" on context and content, one late defective copy; §2.2 (p.174)
    also prints an asterisk beside it whose meaning I did not find explained on the pages I read.
11. **Sharḥ Ḥadīth ʿUlwī** may be a duplicate of nos. 32-34; MK did not see the MS.
12. **Sharḥ al-Basmala copies.** Majlis ff. 332b-51b (about 20 leaves) against Sipahsālār 2930/20 ff. 6b-9b (about 4 leaves):
    very different lengths under one title; MK does not comment. Not encoded as a claim.
13. **Attestation of the Suʾl al-Mulūk request.** The entry states the request of Bāysunghur but not where in the text
    it stands; I marked it `attested`, `medium`, with `subject_self_report` (medium).
14. **Tuḥfa-yi ʿAlāʾī** is a work for a Hanbali patron in the exile years (timeline p.53). MK notes it states the family
    is Shafiʿi and complains of Herat; I recorded these as `work`, not as free testimony of what he held, and said so in
    CL-0336. The dissertation does not call it tailored.
15. **Taqdīm al-ʿAql**'s "Dawlat-i Sayyidiyya-yi Imāmiyya-yi Rażaviyya" is a note on one late copy (Sipahsālār 2930/6);
    MK does not identify it. I did not treat it as the work's dedication.

## Things I did not read or could not resolve

- The Qāżīzāda Rūmī letter (MS Majlis 10196 f. 153a) and the Munshaʾāt itself (§2.1.3.1); EV-0614 records only MK's pointer.
- The passages of the Naẓm al-Durr / Fuṣūṣ / Mafāḥiṣ that supply the 806 date and the cross-references (no page cited).
- What the asterisk on "Sharḥ-i Gulshan-i Rāz*" on p.174 means.
- Any of the reception discussion of the Tamhīd beyond what bears on the work itself: pdf pp.140-145 are a history of
  Imami mystico-philosophical synthesis (Ṭūsī, Qūnavī, the Bahrain school, Ibn Abī Jumhūr, Davānī, Dashtakī, Mīr Dāmād,
  Mullā Ṣadrā). I read it and wrote **no** artifacts from it; it is context for the *historian* act, not biography. The
  one use is MK's placement of the Tamhīd between Ibn ʿArabī and Mullā Ṣadrā (pp.139, 145-146) and his finding of no
  direct influence on Isfahān (p.146), which are in EV-0673/0674 and CL-0331/0332.
