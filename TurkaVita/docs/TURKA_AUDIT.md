## C. Internal inconsistencies in the dissertation the game carries rather than resolves

- *Shaqq-i Qamar* copy date: 27 Feb 1426 (pdf 126) vs 28 Jan 1426 (pdf 128); R4's own calendar check favours January. **Both are kept.**
- *Sharḥ Fuṣūṣ*: 813 (timeline, pdf 52) vs 814/13 June 1411 (entry).
- Timeline pairs one Hijri year with two CE years in five places (808, 813, 817, 833, 834).
- Simnan: c. 1429 (timeline) vs 1430 (text). Scenes say "1429 or 1430".
- *Nafsat II*: "some five years after 830/1427" (p. 77) vs 832–35/1429–32 (fn. 105).
- Fażl Allāh's death: 1393 (diagram A.2) vs 796/1394 (timeline and text).
- *Sharḥ-i Naẓm al-Durr* dated 806/1404 yet cited as citing the *Mafāḥiṣ* (823/1420) (R4 disc. 3; R4's own observation, not MK's).
- Majlis 10196's copying span: "last eight years plus three after death" (≈827–838) vs "828–41" (R3).
- The letters: 46 (p. 150) vs 47 in the list; Sinā 316 holds 45 or "some 40" (R5).
- Folio numbers are unreliable as an exact answer key (overlaps and misprints; R4 disc. 7): the collection puzzle uses only clean ones.

## D. What this changes for the game, and what it does not

- The game **states none of the disputed dates as fact**; it carries `date_kind` (and the hedges) on everything.
- The three-trial reading (1422 / 1426 / 1427) is marked as our reading of MK; **no game text calls anything "the third trial"; the accusations of c. 1422 and 1426 are told as MK tells them (a first charge, and being summoned to be tried again), and 1427 as the purge that followed the attempt on Shāhrukh**.
- Bāysunghur is a court on the board because he is an addressee, not because a patronage start date is sourced.
- The Corbin/Cooper mix-up and the treatment of positions E and F are fixed in the game's own artifacts.
