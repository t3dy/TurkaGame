# TurkaGame

Games and a research pipeline built on **Ṣāʾin al-Dīn ʿAlī ibn Turka Iṣfahānī**
(1369–1432), Chief Judge of Isfahan and the foremost occult philosopher of Timurid Iran —
sourced from extant manuscripts and real scholarship, not invented fantasy.

Three prototypes are planned:
- A **visual novel** through episodes of Ibn Turka's life and its three inquisitions.
- A **roguelike** built on the real "Occult Quintet" of five hierarchically-ordered
  occult sciences (alchemy, talismanry, subjugation, illusionism, trickery).
- An **esoteric scholar career sim** — skill-tree progression through the occult
  sciences, patron relationships, court politics, and the Islamicate economy Ibn Turka
  actually operated in.

Status: early. Research pipeline built first; visual novel is the next prototype to
start. See [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) for the full agent-facing project guide and
[docs/DECISIONS.md](docs/DECISIONS.md) for how that sequencing was decided.

Research grounding: [docs/RESEARCH_BRIEF.md](docs/RESEARCH_BRIEF.md), synthesized from
three papers by Matthew Melvin-Koushki (University of South Carolina), the leading
scholar of Ibn Turka and the Islamicate occult sciences.
