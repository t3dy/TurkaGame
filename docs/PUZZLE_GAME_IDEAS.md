# Puzzle game ideas — the record so far

This file does two jobs. **Part I** is the catalogue: everything puzzle-shaped that exists
in this repo or has been designed for it, with its status and what it rests on. **Part II**
distils `PUZZLERIDEAS.txt` — the design brief for making Ibn Turka's lettrism into
mechanics — into things that can be built, and says in what order.

## How claims are labelled here

The brief asks that every claim be tagged. Six labels are used throughout, and the first is
split in two because the difference matters in *this* repo:

| Tag | Means |
|---|---|
| **SOURCED · repo** | Historically sourced, and the source is in this project — a `portal/data/seed.json` entry (the `PORTAL` kind in game data) or a Melvin-Koushki paper in `research/library/` with a note in `research/notes/` (the `CORPUS` kind). Verifiable here. |
| **SOURCED · brief only** | Historically sourced *according to `PUZZLERIDEAS.txt`*, which cites papers and an Oxford study of Ottoman occultism that are **not** in this repo's corpus. Reported secondhand; not verifiable here until the source is added. Treat as a hypothesis to be tested in play, not a table to be printed. |
| **INTERPRETATION** | A design reading of a sourced fact. Ours. Labelled at the point of use, per the house rule. |
| **EXPERIMENTAL** | A mechanic with no historical claim behind it, tried because it might be interesting. |
| **IMPLEMENTATION** | A fact about the code: what it does, what it was tested to do. |
| **PLAYTEST** | Evidence from a person playing. **There is none in this project yet.** Every "hard", "winnable" or "tuned" below is solver or scripted evidence, and says so. |

Nothing below is claimed to be fun, balanced or canonical.

# Part I — What exists

## 1. Yūsuf Ascent · `games/yusuf-ascent/` · built, live

One folio — Bihzād's *Yūsuf fleeing Zulaykha* (Cairo, Adab Farsi 22, f. 52b) — cut into
**43 nodes (41 with sprites)** and rebuilt three ways. Data layer:
`data/palace.json` (nodes, seven rungs, eight doors, seven locks, per-node `grounds`),
generated by `build_palace.py` from `imagelab/data/regions.json`.

| Prototype | Mechanic | Rests on |
|---|---|---|
| **A · The Seven Doors** (2D) | Click through the door-chain; each door is a lock whose answer is a concept from the portal (mulk, barzakh, malakūt…). | Seven rungs and locks: **INTERPRETATION** of portal entries (`barzakh`, `imaginal-realm`); the door count and the "locked from within, open of themselves" logic: **SOURCED · repo** (Q 12:23 via `palace.json` cards). |
| **B · Station Point** (3D) | The 41 sprites stand at seven depths with perspective compensation k=(D−z)/D so they recompose from one viewpoint only. `checkStationInvariant()` is the numerical self-test. | The one-viewpoint claim: **INTERPRETATION**, labelled in-game. The invariant: **IMPLEMENTATION**, passes. |
| **C · The Ladder** | Drag-sort the elements into rung order. | Same interpretation as A. |

Honesty note carried in the game: no source in the 43-source corpus mentions Bihzād or
Zulaykha at all. The reading is ours and the game says so where the player is judging.

## 2. The Visionary Gallery · `games/visionary-gallery/` · built, live

22 Commons folios fetched with a structured-licence rights gate, measured, auto-decomposed,
made into paper tunnel books and 3D stacks, and made playable (find-the-region and
layer-ordering play per folio). Pipeline: `imagelab/scripts/fetch_commons.py → analyze.py →
papercraft.py → build_gallery.py`.

- The machine **ranks** regions as the hand does (ρ = 0.86) and **finds** 27% of them —
  **IMPLEMENTATION**, measured in `imagelab/data/hand_vs_machine.json`.
- Scholarship panels are exported from the portal at build time
  (`portal/scripts/export_gallery_scholarship.py`) — **SOURCED · repo** by construction.
- As a *puzzle* it is thin: the play is recognition, not reasoning. It is the L4 data-driven
  end of the pipeline, not a mechanic in its own right.

## 3. Abjad Tower · `games/abjad-tower/` · built, live · four modes, six operations

A physics stacker/demolisher (cannon-es + three.js) whose blocks are the 28 letters.

**The one lettrist mechanic already in production:** a block's **mass is its abjad
value** — order exact, ratio compressed by log₁₀ (`build_letters.py`; the compression is
the one declared bend). **SOURCED · repo** (`abjad-numerology`, `ilm-al-huruf`) for the
values and the 28-fold correspondence; the compression is **INTERPRETATION** and is stated
in three places.

| Operation | Effect | Rests on |
|---|---|---|
| Strike ḍarb | thrown stone | control case — **no claim** |
| Talisman ṭilasm | delayed impulse from *inside* the structure | **SOURCED · repo** (`talismanic-science`: force "through the intermediate realm") → delay and interiority are **INTERPRETATION** |
| Invoke the Name ism | every block of one letter goes near-weightless | **SOURCED · repo** (letters ↔ divine names) → "act on the name, act on every instance" is **INTERPRETATION** |
| Reckoning ḥisāb | any stacked run summing to N is annihilated | **SOURCED · repo** (composing words to a sum) → runs ≥ 2 letters is **INTERPRETATION** |
| Doffing khalʿ | block stops colliding 3 s, then returns | **SOURCED · repo** (Mīr Dāmād, *On Doffing*) → physical reading is **INTERPRETATION** |
| Inversion nūr/ẓulma | 14 light and 14 dark letters swap masses | **SOURCED · repo** (the muqaṭṭaʿāt are exactly half the alphabet) → mass-swap is **INTERPRETATION** |

| Mode | Goal | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Demolition hadm | tower below the ring on a budget of 8 | scripted only |
| Raising bināʾ | 18 blocks to 3.6 m, hold 5 s | retuned once, scripted only |
| Extraction istikhrāj | remove every instance of one letter, keep the rest standing | scripted only; no difficulty curve |
| **The Weight of Brackets** kawābīl | the folio's parts as blocks over a void; only the balcony brackets may be fixed in air; reach the turret | deterministic 450-point win (`world.step` loop, AABB gap < 0.12 held 2.5 s). The bracket rule is **SOURCED · repo** (`palace.json`: "carried on nothing"). |

The **Tome** (localStorage) already scores *witnessing something new*, not repetition — the
seed of the tajriba notebook in Part II.

Two verification lessons recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md` and binding on everything below:
**step physics manually to test it** (rAF is throttled during tool calls), and **refuse spawns
that overlap fixed bodies**.

## 4. The Impossible Architect · `games/impossible-architect/` · built, live

A 5 × 7 tile-laying route-builder. Rows are the seven rungs; the folio's 39 non-fixed
elements are the deck; each piece obeys the logic the painting gives it (stairs join any
storey — barzakh; brackets need no support; muqarnas joins diagonally; tile fields spread;
the balcony door is a blind; the turret connects by sight; the cupola is impassable). The
seven doors open only when their lock's answer is standing in what you built — the **same
seven locks as Yūsuf Ascent**, read unchanged from `palace.json`.

- Rules: each **SOURCED · repo** via a `palace.json` card, with the reading marked
  **INTERPRETATION**.
- The centre column is doors-only (the door chain) — a fix found by a solver after v1 was
  trivially winnable and v2 unwinnable. **IMPLEMENTATION**.
- Evidence: a naive greedy solver wins 1 of 8 seeds (seed 99, 25 moves). Human difficulty
  **unmeasured**.

# Part II — The lettrist programme, from `PUZZLERIDEAS.txt`

## The thesis

> Don't make letters another ingredient in the alchemy game. Make Lettrism the programming
> language of the alchemy game.

The chain the brief asks every mechanic to sit on:

```
LETTER → NUMBER → PROPORTION → GLYPH / FORM → MATERIAL EFFECT
```

and, orthogonally, Ibn Turka's three registers of the letter,

```
MENTAL → SPOKEN → WRITTEN
```

which **is** in this repo: the *Mafāḥiṣ* is structured as three "Globes of Light"
corresponding to the mental, written and spoken letter, as an ascent–descent–ascent
(`research/notes/02-prologue-to-pythagorean-renaissance.md`, **SOURCED · repo**).

The intended discovery arc — the player's, not the designer's — runs: *letters have
numbers → numbers affect objects → letters have formal properties → letters correspond to
elements, planets, substances → letters transform between representations → these were one
system all along.*

Two commitments follow, and both are already house style here:

1. **Traditions are networks of relations and operations, not a fixed table.** The brief's
   own caution: there is no single medieval meaning of the alphabet; Ibn ʿArabī, al-Būnī, Ibn
   Turka, the Ḥurūfīs and the Ottomans differ. So the game ships **competing schemes**, hides
   which one is operative in a given puzzle, and makes finding out the play.
2. **The notebook is the progression, and it is taḥqīq.** The portal's `tahqiq-taqlid`
   entry: *taḥqīq* is working a question through to its ground for oneself; *taqlīd* is
   holding it on authority. A correspondence the player has only read is taqlīd; one they
   have tested is taḥqīq. The Tome already pays only for witnessing. Extend it to
   HYPOTHESIS → EXPERIMENT → OBSERVED → CONFIRMED / DISPROVEN. **SOURCED · repo** for the
   axis; the notebook states are **INTERPRETATION**.

## The mechanics, with what each rests on *in this repo*

The brief's 24 numbered ideas, grouped into the ten it asks to investigate (A–J). Grounding
is given as it stands **here**, which is stricter than the brief's own citations.

| | Mechanic | Grounding here | As play |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Alif is singular**: the axis, counts 1, cannot combine with itself; two alifs make a line, not "two alif". | Alif = 1 and "avatar of Oneness": **SOURCED · repo**. Alif's pole position and responsiveness to surroundings (Ibn ʿArabī): **brief only**. | Stacker: the only block allowed to stand on end; a tower built around one alif as spine. Board: alif cannot be an operand, only an origin. |
| **B** | **The dot of bāʾ is a piece.** Form → position → function. | Bāʾ as "first of the dark letters, fountainhead of duality" and the *R. al-Bāʾiyya*: **SOURCED · repo**. The dot discourse (Oğlanlar Şeyhi): **brief only**. | A bāʾ block ships with a movable dot; where the dot sits changes what the block does. Dotless bāʾ is inert. |
| **C** | **Letters have anatomy** — spine, bowl, dots, open/closed, vertical/horizontal — and shape logic. | The *fact* of each glyph's form is observable: **IMPLEMENTATION**. Al-Būnī's *shakl* doctrine: **brief only**. | Closed forms hold, open forms leak, vertical forms stand; two forms can complete one another. |
| **D** | **Temperament**: hot/cold × dry/moist; four elemental letter classes (ṭabāʾiʿ). | **Brief only** (Ibn ʿArabī; Kâtib Çelebi). The repo corpus has none of it. | Ship as *hidden, rival schemes* (see data model). Stacker: complementary neighbours stabilise, like-with-like is brittle. The player discovers which scheme is live. |
| **E** | **Luminous / tenebrous as visibility**, not good/evil. | 14/14 split: **SOURCED · repo**. Visibility reading: **INTERPRETATION**. | Light letters reveal hidden connections on the board; dark letters conceal them. Yūsuf: relations invisible until the right letter is placed. |
| **F** | **Abjad numbers with consequences** beyond mass: size, strength, range, cost, threshold, sequence position, wafq contribution. | Values: **SOURCED · repo**. Each consequence: **INTERPRETATION**. | The alphabet as a resource economy. |
| **G** | **Proportion vs mixture** — two rival explanations of letter efficacy. | Reported by Melvin-Koushki per the brief: **brief only**. The wafq images in the portal catalogue: **SOURCED · repo** (that magic squares are the practice). | The same problem solvable qualitatively (which letters belong together) or quantitatively (which have the right ratio). A puzzle instance secretly picks which explanation is operative. |
| **H** | **Three registers**: mental / spoken / written, ascent and descent. | **SOURCED · repo** (the *Mafāḥiṣ*'s structure). | A block can be raised written → spoken → mental and lowered elsewhere; each register has different affordances; the puzzle is which register needs changing. |
| **I** | **Tajriba notebook**: HYPOTHESIS → EXPERIMENT → OBSERVED → CONFIRMED / DISPROVEN. | Taḥqīq/taqlīd: **SOURCED · repo**. Tajriba-as-proof among the lettermasters: **brief only**. | The progression system. Nothing unlocks by level; capabilities unlock by confirmed correspondence. |
| **J** | **Coincidence of opposites**: solutions that make two incompatible states true at once. | Ibn Turka's lettrism uniting immaterial/material, hidden/manifest, One/many: **SOURCED · repo** (`ilm-al-huruf`, `pythagorean-cosmology`). | Heavier *and* lighter; one block in two places (Doffing already does this); visible *and* hidden; two configurations, one number. The signature late constraint. |

Further ideas from the brief, tagged the same way:

- **Taksīr — break the word apart, permute, recombine, observe.** Taksīr itself: **brief
  only**; jafr as permutation of the alphabet and the *K. al-Jafr*: **SOURCED · repo**
  (`jafr`). A whole puzzle category, and the first floor of the roguelike.
- **The Jafr machine** — permutation search for a hidden name; every run a new letter-space.
  **SOURCED · repo** for jafr as omniscient combinatorial book; the search game is
  **INTERPRETATION**.
- **Magic squares as crafting grids** — rows, columns, diagonals must satisfy a value, but
  the cells are glyphs, so the number is hidden until the player knows abjad. Practice:
  **SOURCED · repo** (al-Būnī's squares; two wafq folios in the image catalogue). Crafting
  reading: **INTERPRETATION**.
- **Transpose** — convert a block between correspondence systems (Fire → ḥāʾ → 8 → Jupiter)
  without creating anything: the *representation* changes. **EXPERIMENTAL**, but it is the
  brief's stated killer mechanic and the one that makes the four systems one.
- **The tower is a word** — vertical sequence read as letters; must be both stable and
  valid; the two goals conflict. **EXPERIMENTAL**.
- **Names as resources** — decompose, calculate, compare; find another entity whose name
  has the required relation. Science of letters *and names*: **SOURCED · repo**
  (`divine-names`). Feeds `CareerSim/` (NPC names that matter mechanically).
- **No spellcasting** — construct the configuration; the effect emerges. Aesthetic rule,
  already how Abjad Tower's operations work.
- **The Seven Doors as seven operations** — Count, Divide (taksīr), Combine, Transform
  (registers), Correspond, Permute, Witness. **INTERPRETATION** over the existing locks;
  drops into Yūsuf Ascent A and the Architect without touching `palace.json`'s nodes.
- **Yūsuf as a program** — assign architectural features letters; shape → letter → number
  → correspondence; ornament turns out to be writing. **EXPERIMENTAL**, and only honest if
  labelled as ours at the point of use.

## Shared data model

The brief asks for one underlying engine. The rule from `CLAUDE.md` still applies — no
shared *code* across game folders until two prototypes are past their first slice — but
shared **data** is already the pattern (`palace.json` feeds three surfaces). So:

1. **`games/abjad-tower/data/letters.json` grows** (generated, `--verify`'d against the
   portal DB as now). New per-letter fields, all **IMPLEMENTATION** facts about the written
   glyph, no occult claim: `form: { orientation: vertical|horizontal|mixed, dots: n,
   dot_position: above|below|none, closed: bool, tail: bool }`. Plus
   `registers: { written: glyph, spoken: translit, mental: name }` — the three levels,
   from the repo's own note.
2. **`games/abjad-tower/data/correspondences.json` — new.** Not a table: a list of
   **schemes**, each with `kind` (PORTAL / CORPUS / REPORTED / INTERPRETATION), its source
   line, and its mapping. At least two rival schemes per domain where the tradition
   disagrees (temperament by Ibn ʿArabī's qualities vs. the cyclic four-element
   assignment; efficacy by mixture vs. by proportion). Puzzles reference a scheme by id and
   may **hide** which one is operative. The player's notebook records their own
   hypotheses per scheme.
3. **The notebook** — one localStorage namespace shared by every game
   (`turka.notebook.v1`), with entries `{ claim, scheme, state:
   HYPOTHESIS|EXPERIMENT|OBSERVED|CONFIRMED|DISPROVEN, witnessed_in: [game, seed] }`.
   Abjad Tower's Tome becomes a view of it.
4. **`palace.json` unchanged.** The seven-operations reading of the doors is an overlay
   file, not a rewrite.
