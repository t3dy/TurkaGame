Archive

Every version, kept playable

Nothing here is deleted when it is superseded. When a change is substantial enough that someone might want to compare — a rewrite of the writing, a new engine — the previous state is frozen at its own address and stays live. This page is the index of all of it.

Ibn Turka: The Occult Court — career roguelike

  • Career Sim — current slice 2 · 2026-08-31

    All five life-phases, 70 encounters, career systems (obligations, patron contracts, compounding exposure), 14×9 ending matrix, historical-term glossing, and “The Attested Life” comparison at the end. New in this build: publish a finished run as a permanent scholarly witness — a public page plus separate private links for the player’s and the scholar’s hand.

The Visual Novel

  • Version 3 — current 2026-08-30

    The writing audit build: all 87 choice options rewritten to roughly a third of their previous length and audited to name a real person, text or institution wherever the research supports one. Eight endings; themed ending journal.

  • Version 2 superseded

    Same structure as v3, with the longer two-to-four-sentence option reasoning that the writing audit found had drifted generic. Frozen for comparison.

  • Version 1 superseded

    The first playable build: short choice labels only, before per-option reasoning text existed at all.

  • Version 3 (frozen snapshot) snapshot

    A pinned copy of the v3 build, kept alongside the live one.
