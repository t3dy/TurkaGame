Timeline

50 dated events, 950–2025

Four interleaved threads: Ibn Turka's own biography; the texts and discoveries around him (his own works, the Samarkand Observatory, the sources his lettrism draws on); the historiography of science — when and how modern scholarship came to recover this material; and his relationship to European Renaissance magi — not documented direct contact (there was none), but the explicit comparisons Matthew Melvin-Koushki draws to Cusa, Pico, Bruno, and Dee, plus surrounding context.

Every event is tagged with how well-grounded it is — see the legend at the bottom. This isn't decoration: several of the historiography entries are honestly marked HISTORIOGRAPHY / low-confidence because they're drawn from general knowledge of the field rather than a source document this project actually has in hand.
