The Ibn Turka Knowledge Portal

A research portal dedicated to Ṣāʾin al-Dīn ʿAlī ibn Turka Iṣfahānī — Chief Judge of Isfahan, and the most systematic occult philosopher of Timurid Iran.

This portal asks one question in many forms: where does Ibn Turka meet the wider world of Islamicate occult science, and what changes when he does? The Intersections below are its centre — each takes a general context and follows Ibn Turka into it. Entries for the figures, concepts, texts and institutions around him fill in the rest.
