Treatise on the Barzakh

Risalat al-Barzakh. Ibn Turka's dedicated treatise on the intermediate realm (barzakh) where causation operates. Expounds the metaphysical framework grounding all lettrist operations: how divine names become manifest in forms, how letters function as operative principles, how human will participates in cosmic causation through knowledge of the barzakh.

primary-sourcebarzakhmetaphysicscausation

The Treatise on the Barzakh is Ibn Turka's specialized exposition of the ontological and operative theory of the intermediate realm. Where the Kitab al-Mafahis provides comprehensive systematization of all lettrist sciences, the Barzakh treatise focuses on the metaphysical domain where all operations occur. It establishes: (1) Ontological status of barzakh—it is not merely symbolic or psychological but an actual realm possessing objective reality. (2) The barzakh as causal nexus—all causation flows through the intermediate domain; material effects originate in barzakh-level operations. (3) Human participation—the soul operates in the barzakh; through knowledge of letters and divine names, the will can align with cosmic principles and effect change. (4) Letters as barzakh-entities—letters are primarily barzakh realities; their material expression (ink, stone, parchment) is secondary manifestation. The Barzakh treatise is essential reading for understanding the operational framework underlying all talismanic and lettrist practice.
