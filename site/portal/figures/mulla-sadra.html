Mulla Sadra

Safavid philosopher (1571–1635) who received and systematized Ibn Turka's tradition. Synthesized Pythagorean occultism with peripatetic philosophy. Founder of Transcendent Theosophy. Demonstrates transmission of Ibn Turka's system into early modern Islamic philosophy.

philosophermetaphysicssafavidsuccessor

Mulla Sadra inherited Ibn Turka's intellectual tradition—Pythagorean mathematics, Illuminationism, systematic occultism—and synthesized it with post-Avicennian philosophy. His Hikmat al-Muta'aliya represents mature development of Ibn Turka's themes: unity of being, mathematics in cosmology, mystical knowledge. Proves Ibn Turka's system was not marginal but generative tradition producing major philosophical achievements into 17th century.
