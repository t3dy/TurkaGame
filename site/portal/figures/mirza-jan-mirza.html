Mirza Jan (Safavid Scholar)

Safavid-era scholar who studied and transmitted Ibn Turka's manuscripts. Ensured survival and canonization of Ibn Turka's works in Safavid court libraries. Represents the scholarly transmission chain keeping Ibn Turka's system alive.

scholartransmittersafavid

Mirza Jan exemplifies the transmission network that kept Ibn Turka's tradition alive from 15th into 16th century. As a collector and copyist of Ibn Turka's manuscripts, he contributed to their preservation and circulation within Safavid intellectual circles.
