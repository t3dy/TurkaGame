Mamluk Cairo Context

Cairo was the intellectual and political center of the Mamluk Sultanate (1250–1517). Akhlati and other Ibn Turka's predecessors worked in Cairo courts. Mamluk patronage of scholars influenced Timurid court structures.

Mamluk Cairo was the largest Muslim city and intellectual center. It provided patronage for scholars including Akhlati. The Mamluk court model influenced how Timurid courts operated.
