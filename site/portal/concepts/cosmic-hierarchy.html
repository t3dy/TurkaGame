Cosmic Hierarchy

Ibn Turka's cosmological model: intelligible realm (divine names), imaginal realm (subtle forms), sensible realm (material existence). Levels are interconnected; operations in imaginal affect sensible.

cosmologyhierarchylevels

Creation unfolds through three levels interconnected by causation. The imaginal realm is key: where letters and divine names exercise force.
