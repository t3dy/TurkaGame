Timurid Court Patronage

The institutional and political context enabling Ibn Turka and Isfahan Circle. Timurid rulers patronized scholars, established courts as intellectual centers, created conditions for systematic philosophical innovation.

patronagetimuridcourtpolitical

Timurid dynasty (1370–1506) under figures like Shahrukh actively patronized scholars and artists. Courts in Samarkand, Isfahan, Yazd became magnets for intellectuals. This patronage enabled Ibn Turka's work and the Isfahan Circle's activities.
