Occult Philosophy

Islamic tradition positioning occult sciences as rigorous, systematic inquiry into hidden principles of creation. Distinguished from superstition by mathematical grounding and philosophical coherence. Ibn Turka's system is exemplar.

occultphilosophysystematic

Occult philosophy in Islamic context means systematic inquiry into principles underlying manifest reality—what is hidden (batin) as opposed to manifest (zahir). Not superstition but rigorous science grounded in mathematics and metaphysics.
