Universal Principles

Ibn Turka's claim that lettrist science is universal—applicable to all creation because all creation is constituted through letters and divine names. Not provincial tradition but systematic account of reality's fundamental structure.

universalityprinciplesystem

Ibn Turka's system claims universality: letters and divine names are the principles through which all being unfolds. Therefore, knowledge of letters is knowledge of creation itself. This universality distinguishes Ibn Turka's system from folk magic or regional practices.
