TurkaGame

Games from the world of Ibn Turka, 1369–1432

Prototypes built from extant manuscripts and real scholarship on Ṣāʾin al-Dīn ʿAlī ibn Turka Iṣfahānī — Chief Judge of Isfahan and the foremost occult philosopher of Timurid Iran, whose Pythagorean lettrist cosmology shaped imperial culture across the Timurid, Aqquyunlu, Safavid, Uzbek, Ottoman, and Mughal courts.

Prototypes

  • Visual novel playable prototype — 40 branching life-choices, an Occult Quintet skill tree, multiple endings. See the full pitch or play it now.
  • Roguelike design stage
  • Esoteric scholar career sim design stage

Research pipeline built first; the visual novel is now a playable prototype — the full 40-choice, 8-act system with a working skill tree and computed endings, though its scene text is still thin placeholder prose, not final writing. See The Visual Novel for the full feature pitch.
